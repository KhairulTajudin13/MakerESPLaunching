# Maker ESP32 — Full Launch Roadmap

**Launch:** 16 Sep 2026, exact time TBC (target ~3:00 PM) · **Offer:** 20% off, 16–29 Sep, automatic, no code
**Primary audience:** Tertiary students learning ESP32 for the first time. Lecturers are a channel to reach them, not a separate target audience.
**Launch scope:** Malaysia-first. SG/TH/VN follow post-launch.

## Phase 3 — Launch Window (16–29 Sep)

- **16 Sep, 3:00 PM (confirmed):** product page + homepage banner go live simultaneously — anchor moment for all other channels. Coordination owner: **EDU MY** (go/hold call). Full launch day checklist built separately.
- Homepage banner — built and published by **Marketing Team**, matches existing homepage carousel slide format (headline left, product photo right, CTA pill)
- Countdown timer — **post-launch only**, runs 16→29 Sep counting down time left on the offer (standard practice, not a pre-launch hype countdown)
- All channels fire same day: Facebook, Instagram, Telegram (5 groups), newsletter

### Progression content (IoT + FYP — robotics dropped, didn't fit naturally)
Each ties to a real tutorial publishing that same window, not a generic example:

| Post | Date | Centerpiece tutorial |
|---|---|---|
| IoT progression | 17 Sep | #28 Smart Home Dashboard |
| FYP progression | 24 Sep | #33 Smart Weather Station (published launch day, revisited here) |

- Remaining daily tutorial publishes (including #22 Motion Detector Alert, 20 Sep) get a lightweight "new tutorial live" post using the existing tutorial card template — no dedicated content brief needed
- Lecturers share with their cohorts during this window — **organic, not scripted**: no fixed ask on *how* they share, just the objective explained. They're given the datasheet, Getting Started guide, and NodeMCU comparison table — free board, permanently theirs, no need to return. Ask is light — verbal mention in class (after their own testing) or sharing on their own platform if they have one. Timing fully lecturer-paced, no synchronized push, no pressure — some may need student help to test. **Incentive:** token of appreciation planned (Cytron merchandise), details TBC. Track "Shared publicly (Y/N)" per lecturer for Phase 4 evergreen proof sourcing.
- **Launch-day nudge message — drafted and saved, ready to send when launch goes live:**
  > "Hi [Lecturer Name], Maker ESP32 is officially live today. Thank you again for taking the time to test it and share your feedback earlier — it genuinely helped us get this right. No pressure at all, but if you'd like to mention it to your students or share it on your own platform, here's the link: [product page link]. There's a 20% launch discount running until 29 September if that's useful to pass along. Either way, really glad you got to try it early, and thank you for being part of this. Best, [Your name], Cytron Technologies"
- **Tutorial-to-community funnel:** starting the week before mid-window (~15–21 Sep), every published tutorial includes a banner/CTA at the end directing readers into the Telegram ESP32 Community group. Grows the audience the mid-window reminder lands in front of, rather than posting to a static group. Also broadens reach — pulls in general ESP32/maker audiences, not just people already following Cytron's channels.
- **Mid-window reminder (~22–23 Sep):** Urgency + proof, not a bare reminder — draws on stock momentum (only if genuinely true at the time — avoid false scarcity claims; framing like "Maker ESP32 is safely shipped to customers" works well without the risk), new tutorials published since launch (including EDU MY's own additions), and any lecturer feedback picked up by then. Scaled-back channel: Telegram, ESP Makers Malaysia group only. Visual + text — visual produced in-house, copy to be requested closer to the date.
- **Final reminder (~27–28 Sep):** Standard format — visual + caption as usual for FB, IG, and Telegram (not visual-only). Newsletter: text-primary, visual kept minimal. Channels: ESP32 Community Telegram (383 members as of this point) + Newsletter, possibly FB/IG.
- Tutorial publishing continues — remaining 15 roll out through the window, 1/day, **published by Marketing Team**, each with a **final check/audit before going live** (not a straightforward auto-publish)
- Daily monitoring — first-time buyers ask setup/compatibility questions, respond same-day
